### Howdy, I'm Daniel!

Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering (Controls) at the **University of Oklahoma**. I build controllers and estimators that let drones and ground robots operate on their own.

**Current work**
- **DATUM:** learning-based MPC for a lift-plus-cruise VTOL that learns corrections only where they help; real time (~30 ms/step) and validated against ArduPilot SITL
- **Dissertation:** estimation and predictive control for drone teams without GPS, on a 5-drone fleet
- **Risk-aware autonomy:** mission planning for medical-delivery UAVs (CARA) and navigation around people for construction-site robots (FRANCIS)
